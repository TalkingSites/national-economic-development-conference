<div class="prose lede">

Registrations are open for the 2026 National Economic Development Conference,
held in Hobart (Nipaluna) with the theme *Place to Prosperity: Innovation,
Transition and Investment Attraction*.

</div>

<div class="prose">

From 13 to 15 October, the City of Hobart hosts Australia's economic developers
for a dynamic three day event. The conference explores how places can position
themselves to prosper in a time of economic transition by leveraging innovation,
attracting investment, and building resilient, inclusive local economies.

Keynote presentations, study tours, panel discussions, interactive workshops, a
live debate and breakout sessions feature experts sharing innovative thinking
and global best practice, to support economic developers meeting the challenges
of today amidst transitioning industries and communities.

</div>
